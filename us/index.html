Questions

Short, honest answers.

Who is ESTRIQ for?

Independent landlords with 1 to 30 units: single-family homes, condos, small multifamily and commercial units. For anyone who tracks their rentals in a spreadsheet, a banking app or their head – and wants to know more precisely.

How is ESTRIQ different from a spreadsheet?

A spreadsheet shows what you type in. ESTRIQ does the math: cash flow after the mortgage, returns, loan balance, equity built – automatically, every month, for every property. And it checks in on rent day to ask whether the rent has arrived. A spreadsheet doesn’t.

Is ESTRIQ property management software?

No, and that’s on purpose. ESTRIQ doesn’t collect rent, screen tenants or list vacancies. It’s the cockpit above all that: the numbers you care about as an owner, without the overhead of software built for 300 units.

Where is my data stored – and who can see it?

Your data is stored encrypted on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU), and belongs to your account only. Payments run through Stripe – we never see your card details. ESTRIQ is operated by a company in Germany, so European data protection law (GDPR) applies. Details are in the privacy notice below.

Do I need an app from the App Store?

No. ESTRIQ runs in your browser. On iPhone, iPad and desktop you can add it to your home screen in two taps – then it looks and opens like an app.

When does ESTRIQ launch in the US?

We’re opening step by step so every new user gets proper support. People on the waitlist get access first, in the order they signed up. We’ll email you when your access is ready – nothing else.

Does ESTRIQ handle US taxes or state rules?

Not at launch. ESTRIQ shows your numbers; it doesn’t give tax or legal advice. US-specific features such as closing costs, property tax and a depreciation overview are planned for later.
